The Colors of Navratri

Colour is part of how many devotees mark each night. Here is what the colours stand for, and why no single chart is the rule.

Colour in Hindu traditions

Colours carry meaning in Hindu life: saffron for renunciation and fire, red for auspiciousness and power, white for purity, yellow for turmeric and knowledge. During Navratri some devotees wear a colour assigned to each night.

These meanings are symbolic readings, not religious laws. Practice is a matter of community and personal choice.
