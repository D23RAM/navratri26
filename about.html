About this project

NAVARATRI is an educational website about the festival and the nine forms of Maa Durga.

Purpose

The site helps visitors learn who the nine Devis are, what their symbols mean, how colours are used during the festival and how the nine nights are understood. It was built as a final project for the course WDD231, Web Frontend Development.

How to explore

Start with The Nine Devis to search, filter and save favourites. Visit The Colors for the colour charts and Navratri for the calendar, timeline and symbolism library.

Respect and variation

Hindu traditions are diverse. Stories, iconography, mantras and practices differ between regions, communities and texts. Where the site gives one version, it says so. The site has no religious authority; for guidance on observance, speak with a pandit or your local temple.

The pictures of the Devis on this site are symbolic emblems made for the project, not portraits, so that no sacred form is drawn inaccurately. Attributions lists every source.
